# Demo Bank - Corporate Payments: Cut-off Times and Limits

_Illustrative policy for a fictional bank, written for this demo. Not regulatory guidance._

## Scope

This guide covers outward NEFT, RTGS and IMPS payments initiated by corporate customers through the Demo Bank corporate banking channel, host-to-host files and the branch.

## NEFT

NEFT is available 24x7 on all days of the year and settles in half-hourly batches. There is no minimum or maximum amount for NEFT; the customer's own daily transaction limit applies. A payment submitted after a batch closes goes into the next batch. NEFT credits that cannot be applied to the beneficiary account are returned to the remitter within two hours of the batch settlement.

## RTGS

RTGS is available 24x7 for real-time gross settlement of high-value payments. The minimum RTGS amount is Rs 2,00,000; there is no upper limit. Corporate RTGS instructions received through host-to-host files are processed in the order received. An RTGS payment that cannot be credited is returned to the remitter within one hour of receipt by the beneficiary bank.

## IMPS

IMPS is available 24x7 for immediate credit up to Rs 5,00,000 per transaction. Corporate users must register beneficiaries before their first IMPS payment; a newly registered beneficiary is subject to a cooling period of four hours during which the limit is capped at Rs 50,000.

## Channel cut-offs for same-day value

For payments initiated through the branch, the cut-off for same-day processing is 4:30 PM on working days. Bulk payment files uploaded through the corporate channel after 8:00 PM are value-dated the next calendar day unless the customer has opted for 24x7 file processing.

## Daily limits

The default daily outward payment limit for a corporate customer is Rs 10 crore across all channels. Limits above Rs 10 crore need approval from the Relationship Manager and the Credit Risk team, and are reviewed every twelve months.
