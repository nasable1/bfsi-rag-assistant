# Demo Bank - Corporate Onboarding and KYC

_Illustrative policy for a fictional bank, written for this demo. Not regulatory guidance._

## Documents for a private limited company

A private limited company must provide its certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, PAN of the company, a board resolution authorising the account opening and naming the authorised signatories, and the officially valid documents of each authorised signatory.

## Beneficial ownership

The bank identifies every natural person who ultimately owns or controls more than 10 percent of the company's shares, capital or profits. Where no such person exists, the senior managing official is recorded as the beneficial owner.

## Risk categories and re-KYC

Customers are classified as low, medium or high risk at onboarding. Re-KYC is due every ten years for low-risk customers, every eight years for medium-risk customers and every two years for high-risk customers. A change in authorised signatories triggers an immediate KYC update for the new signatories.

## Channel access

Corporate banking channel access is activated only after KYC is complete and the board resolution is verified. Each authorised user receives an individual login; shared logins are not permitted.
