# Demo Bank - Payment Exceptions, Returns and Complaints

_Illustrative policy for a fictional bank, written for this demo. Not regulatory guidance._

## Failed and returned payments

When an outward payment fails, the amount is credited back to the customer's debit account. For NEFT, the refund is made within two hours of the return being received. For IMPS, a transaction that is debited but not credited to the beneficiary is reversed automatically by the next business day.

## Duplicate payment detection

A payment with the same debit account, beneficiary account, amount and customer reference as another payment in the previous 48 hours is held as a possible duplicate. The maker is notified and the payment is released only after a checker confirms that it is intentional.

## Compensation for delays

If a failed IMPS transaction is not reversed by the next business day, the customer is compensated at Rs 100 per day of delay. Compensation is credited automatically without the customer needing to claim it.

## Complaints

Customers can raise a payment complaint through the corporate channel, by email to the relationship team, or at the branch. Each complaint receives a reference number. Complaints are acknowledged within one business day and resolved within seven business days; unresolved complaints are escalated to the Nodal Officer.
